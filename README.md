A Car Sales Dashboard in Power BI provides detailed insights into automotive sales performance, including:  

1. **YTD Metrics**: Year-to-date total sales, average price, and total cars sold.  
2. **Weekly Trends**: Visualization of sales trends over weeks to identify demand fluctuations.  
3. **Sales by Body Type**: Breakdown of sales performance across different car body types (SUV, sedan, hatchback, etc.).  
4. **Sales by Color**: Analysis of car sales based on popular color preferences.  
5. **Regional Performance**: Sales distribution by dealer regions to identify top-performing areas.  
6. **Company vs. Sales Trends**: Comparative analysis of sales performance by car brands.  

Interactive visuals like pie chart, bar charts, and line graphs enable stakeholders to monitor performance and identify growth opportunities.
